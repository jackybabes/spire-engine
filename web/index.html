Play on iPhone or iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Spire Viewer from your home screen: it runs full screen, without Safari's bars.
  4. Turn your phone sideways: the game plays in landscape.

Runs you play in the home-screen app are kept there, apart from the ones in Safari.
